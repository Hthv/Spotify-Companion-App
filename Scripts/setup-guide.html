01
Part one

Install
the app.

The app isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Drag Spotify Companion into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.
    Don't choose Move to Bin. Nothing is wrong with the app, it just isn't notarized by Apple.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the message about Spotify Companion and click Open Anyway.
    Enter your Mac password if asked, then click Open Anyway once more.
  4. When asked whether Spotify Companion may control Spotify, click Allow.
    That's how play, pause, skip and the current song work.

Client ID.

Spotify only lets apps like this one reach your account through a key you create yourself. It's free.

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Premium account.
  2. First time? Accept the Developer Terms and confirm your email if Spotify asks.
  3. Click Create app and fill in the form:

  4. Tick the terms box and click Save.
  5. On your app's page, click Settings and copy the Client ID.

03
Part three

Connect.

Paste your Client ID into the app and say yes in your browser.

  1. Open Spotify Companion and click the person icon at the top right.
  2. Paste your Client ID into the Client ID box.
  3. Click Connect. Your browser opens Spotify's sign-in page.
  4. Check it's your account and click Agree.
  5. The tab says “Connected. You can close this tab.” Close it and go back to the app.
    The person icon fills in, and Home and Search show your music.
  6. If macOS asks to use your keychain, enter your Mac login password and click Always Allow.
    That's where the app keeps you signed in, so you don't have to connect again.

If something goes wrong

Fixes.

  1. “INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI”
    The Redirect URI doesn't match. In your app's Settings, click Edit, set it to exactly http://127.0.0.1:43821/callback, and Save.
  2. “User not registered” or a 403 error
    This Spotify account isn't the app's owner and hasn't been added under User Management (see Share your Client ID below).
  3. The app shows a Reconnect card
    An update added a feature that needs a new permission. Click Reconnect, or Disconnect then Connect again from the person icon.
  1. The browser can't connect after Agree
    Keep Spotify Companion open while you sign in. If another app is using port 43821, quit it and try again.
  2. Buttons do nothing, or no song shows
    Open the Spotify desktop app. If you clicked Don't Allow, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation › Spotify Companion.
  3. “Quota exceeded” or too many requests
    Spotify limits developer apps. Wait a few minutes and try again.
Optional
Share your Client ID.
Up to five people, each with their own Premium. It's usually simpler for everyone to make their own (Part two).
  1. Open your app on developer.spotify.com/dashboard.
  2. Click User Management.
  3. Add their name and their Spotify account's email. Save.
  4. Send them your Client ID. They follow Part three.
Spotify Companion is an independent app and isn't made, endorsed or supported by Spotify. Your login stays in your Mac's keychain, and the app never sees your Spotify password.
